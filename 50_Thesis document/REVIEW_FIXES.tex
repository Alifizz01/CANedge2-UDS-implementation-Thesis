\begin{table}[H]
\caption[Validation status of each decoded signal]{Validation status of every signal recovered in
this work. The distinction matters: only class~A is confirmed against a reference that is
independent of the decoding, and only class~A may be described as \emph{validated}.}
\label{tab:validation_classes}
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{2.1cm}p{3.5cm}p{7.3cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Class} & \textbf{Signals} & \textbf{Basis, and what it does \emph{not} establish} \\
\midrule
\textbf{A -- directly validated} &
\hex{7448} operation mode; \hex{F40D} speed &
Agree with a directly observable external fact: the vehicle was switched on and stationary
throughout. These are the only two signals with a reference wholly independent of the CAN data. \\[2pt]

\textbf{B -- indirectly validated} &
\hex{1E3B} pack voltage; \hex{1E40}$+$ cell voltages; \hex{1E33}/\hex{1E34} extremes &
Mutually consistent under a physical constraint that does not involve the decoding:
$V_\mathrm{pack}/\overline{V}_\mathrm{cell}=95.95$--$96.08$, and the independently
sampled cells (3.888--3.890\,V) lie strictly inside the BMS-reported extremes
(3.881--3.894\,V). Confirms the scalings are \emph{mutually} correct; does not fix an
absolute offset common to all of them. \\[2pt]

& \hex{2A0B}, \hex{1EAE}$+$, \hex{7426}, \hex{1E0E}, \hex{1E0F} temperatures &
All cluster at 16.0--16.4\,\textdegree C and the extremes bracket the sampled points; broadly
consistent with the outdoor reading (16.5--18.0\,\textdegree C). No calibrated thermometer was
placed in the pack, so this is consistency, not calibration. \\[2pt]

\textbf{C -- plausible but unconfirmed} &
\hex{028C} SOC &
72.8--73.2\,\% is plausible and internally steady, \textbf{but no dashboard photograph or
independent SOC reading was recorded for these sessions}. It cannot be called validated. \\[2pt]

& \hex{1E1B}/\hex{1E1C} limits; \hex{1620} PTC; \hex{2609} outdoor T; \hex{295A} odometer &
Physically plausible magnitudes, no independent reference taken. \\[2pt]

& \hex{2AF7} 12\,V voltage &
$\approx$14.7\,V decoded from the \emph{first frame only}; the two bytes the formula needs
happen to fall inside it. Consistent with an active DC/DC, but the remaining 20 bytes of the
record were never seen, so the field offsets are unverified. \\[2pt]

& \hex{1E3D} pack current &
First frame carries only the top three data bytes, giving $-4.96$\,A or $-2.40$\,A depending on
the last-seen byte. Plausible for a stationary vehicle; \textbf{not recoverable} without ISO-TP. \\[2pt]

\textbf{D -- invalid / unexplained} &
\hex{1EA0}, \hex{1EA3}, \hex{1EA7} cells 97/100/104 &
Constant raw \hex{0FFE} in 53/53 reads. \hex{0FFE} sits one below the 12-bit all-ones code:
interpreted here as a reserved \emph{not available} marker for a slot the pack does not
populate. Excluded, not rescaled. \\[2pt]

& \hex{0800} A/C compressor &
Answers, varies over six distinct values, but the source list gives no equation and only the
first frame was captured. Uninterpreted. \\[2pt]

& \hex{2AB2}, \hex{2AB8} energy content &
No response and no negative response code, while \hex{2AF7} on the \emph{same} ECU (\hex{710})
answered in the same session. The ECU is reachable; these identifiers are not. Note that no
extended session was opened on \hex{710}, so a session-state cause cannot be excluded. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
